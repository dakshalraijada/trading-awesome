# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who participates in issues, pull requests, and reviews is expected to follow it.

## Additional expectations for this list

Many contributors represent the companies they submit. That is welcome, provided it happens in the open:

- **Disclose your affiliation** with any service you add, update, or comment on.
- **No astroturfing.** Do not use multiple accounts, coordinated accounts, or paid third parties to submit, endorse, or defend a listing.
- **No attacks on competitors.** Factual corrections with sources are welcome; disparaging another service, filing bad-faith reports, or attempting to get a competitor removed without evidence is not.
- **No offers of payment, gifts, or tokens** to maintainers or contributors in exchange for listings, placement, or reviews.
- **No solicitation.** Do not use issues or pull requests to advertise, promote tokens, or contact users.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, close issues and pull requests, remove listings, and block accounts that violate this code of conduct. Undisclosed affiliation or astroturfing leads to removal of the related listings.

To report a conduct problem privately, use [private reporting](https://github.com/lentrade/trading-awesome/security/advisories/new) and start the title with **"Conduct:"**. For abusive content anywhere on GitHub, you can also use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are handled confidentially.
